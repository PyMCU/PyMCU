## How this file is maintained

Sponsors at $10/month and above are listed here by GitHub handle or name, as promised in the
sponsor tiers. Ecosystem Partners also get their logo on the main [README](README.md). If you
sponsor and would rather not be listed, say so and the entry is removed.
